Pakistan

Introduction

Pakistan is a country in South Asia. Its capital is Islamabad and its largest city is Karachi.

History

Pakistan became an independent country on 14 August 1947.

Geography

Pakistan has mountains in the north, plains in the middle and a coastline in the south.

Quick Facts

  • Capital: Islamabad
  • Largest City: Karachi
  • Language: Urdu, English
  • Independence: 14 August 1947
