Panda

The giant panda is a black and white bear that lives in the bamboo forests of China. It spends most of the day eating bamboo and can eat many kilograms of it every day. Pandas are calm and shy animals, and they are a symbol of wildlife protection around the world.

Quick Facts

  • Habitat: bamboo forests of China
  • Food: mostly bamboo
  • Group: lives alone
  • Life span: about 20 years
