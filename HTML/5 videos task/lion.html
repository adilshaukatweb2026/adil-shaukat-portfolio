Lion

The lion is a large wild cat and one of the most famous animals in the world. It is called the king of the jungle because of its strength and its thick mane. Lions live in groups called prides, and the lionesses do most of the hunting.

Quick Facts

  • Habitat: grasslands and savannas of Africa
  • Food: meat (zebra, buffalo, deer)
  • Group name: pride
  • Life span: about 10 to 14 years in the wild
