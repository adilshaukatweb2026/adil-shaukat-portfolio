Top 5 Animals

1. Lion

The lion is a large and powerful wild cat, often called the king of the jungle. Lions live in groups called prides and hunt together to catch their food. They are found mostly in the grasslands of Africa. A male lion is easy to recognise because of its thick mane. Read More    Explore


2. Elephant

The elephant is the largest land animal in the world. It has a long trunk, big ears, and strong tusks. Elephants are very intelligent and live in families led by the oldest female. They eat plants and can drink many litres of water every day. Read More    Explore


3. Eagle

The eagle is a large and powerful bird of prey with sharp eyesight. It has strong wings, a curved beak, and sharp claws for catching food. Eagles build their nests very high on cliffs and tall trees. They can spot a small animal from far away while flying high in the sky. Read More    Explore


4. Dolphin

The dolphin is a smart and friendly sea animal that lives in oceans around the world. It swims very fast and can jump high above the water. Dolphins talk to each other using clicks and whistles, and they usually live in groups called pods. They eat fish and squid. Read More    Explore


5. Panda

The giant panda is a black and white bear that lives in the bamboo forests of China. It spends most of the day eating bamboo, and can eat many kilograms of it every day. Pandas are calm and shy animals, and they are a symbol of wildlife protection around the world. Read More    Explore
