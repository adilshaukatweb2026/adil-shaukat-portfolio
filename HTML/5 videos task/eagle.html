Eagle

The eagle is a large and powerful bird of prey with very sharp eyesight. It has strong wings, a curved beak, and sharp claws for catching food. Eagles build their nests on high cliffs and tall trees, and they can spot a small animal from far away while flying high in the sky.

Quick Facts

  • Habitat: mountains, forests, and near rivers
  • Food: fish, rabbits, and small animals
  • Group: usually lives alone or in pairs
  • Life span: about 20 to 30 years
