Elephant

The elephant is the largest land animal in the world. It has a long trunk, big ears, and strong tusks. Elephants are very intelligent and live in families led by the oldest female.

Quick Facts

  • Habitat: forests and savannas of Africa and Asia
  • Food: grass, leaves, fruit, and bark
  • Group name: herd
  • Life span: about 60 to 70 years
