Dolphin

The dolphin is a smart and friendly sea animal that lives in oceans around the world. It swims very fast and can jump high above the water. Dolphins talk to each other using clicks and whistles, and they usually live in groups called pods.

Quick Facts

  • Habitat: oceans and seas
  • Food: fish and squid
  • Group name: pod
  • Life span: about 20 to 40 years
