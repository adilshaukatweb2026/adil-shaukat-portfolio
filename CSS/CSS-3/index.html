OCEAN FREIGHT

Ocean freight is the method of transporting commercial goods, bulk commodities, or personal items across international waters using cargo ships.

AIR FREIGHT

Air freight refers to the shipment of goods via commercial or dedicated cargo aircraft. It is one of the fastest and most reliable methods of international and domestic logistics.

STREET FREIGHT

Street freight (often called urban freight or local road freight) refers to the transport, delivery, and collection of goods on city streets, covering local distribution, last-mile delivery.
